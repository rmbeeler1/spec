# v0.4 "Produce" release report

Date: October 13, 2026. Built against the owner-confirmed `docs/acceptance/v0.4.md`. Per the release gate, v0.4.0 is NOT tagged yet: the manual boxes below are yours, then say the word.

## What was built

Everything a script needs once it leaves the desk and goes through production hands.

- **Revision mode** (Production menu): start a set, and every edit you make gets its colored asterisk in the margin, in the editor and in the PDF. End the set when the draft goes out; sets are listed with dates. Omit Scene parks a whole scene in the stash behind an OMITTED heading that keeps its number; Un-omit brings it back exactly; Cmd+Z reverses either.
- **Page locking** (Production > Lock / Unlock Pages): page 12 stays page 12 forever. New material becomes 12A, 12B; cuts leave pages short. Unedited, the locked layout is line-identical to what you locked, (MORE) and (CONT'D) included, proven across all 125 pages of Big Fish.
- **Scene numbering** (Production > Number Scenes): real `#1#` markers in your Fountain file, shown everywhere and printed in both PDF margins. Lock numbers to give inserted scenes 12A-style numbers. Renumber is one undo step.
- **Dual dialogue** (Edit > Toggle Dual Dialogue, Cmd+Alt+D): two speeches side by side in the editor, the page, and the PDF, never split across a page.
- **Reports** (sixth view, Cmd+Alt+6): scenes, characters, locations, INT/EXT, time of day, writing history; each exports as .txt or .csv.
- **Writing stats and goals** (Production > Writing Goal): per-day words and pages, live in the status bar. Imports, restores and undo never count; deleting never counts against you.
- **Templates**: Stage Play, Audio Drama, Comic, with their own layouts driven by a visible "Format:" key on the title page.

## What to click to try it

1. Open your script, Production > Number Scenes, then export a PDF: numbers in both margins.
2. Production > Revision Sets > Start Set. Edit three lines; watch the blue asterisks. Export the PDF.
3. Put the cursor in a scene you hate: Production > Omit Scene. Cmd+Z. Breathe. Redo, then Un-omit.
4. Production > Lock / Unlock Pages, then write a new paragraph mid-script and watch the page map grow an A-page instead of renumbering.
5. Click into the second of two back-to-back speeches, Cmd+Alt+D.
6. Cmd+Alt+6 for Reports; set a daily goal under Production > Writing Goal.

## How it was verified

- 255 automated tests, including: locked pagination reproducing Big Fish to the line with no edits; A-page conventions under simulated insertions; the exported PDF read back with pdf.js to confirm asterisks, scene numbers and "1A." labels actually print; dual-pair keep-together; report tallies summing to scene counts; writing-stat accumulation; sidecar round-trips for every new block.
- A 32/32 live drive of the built app (`docs/evidence/v0.4-live-drive.txt`, screenshots `v0.4-*.png`): locked A-numbering while typing, goal tracking, dual columns measured pixel-aligned, revision asterisks, omit/un-omit with undo pairing both ways, locked A-page divider appearing mid-typing, and a full relaunch proving marks, locks and the active set come back.
- The drive caught and fixed a real bug: text typed with a trailing space lost its revision mark on reload (anchors are now whitespace-normalized, with a regression test).

## Known gaps and honest notes

- Locked pages that start mid-speech lock to the line; A-page overflow breaks are simpler than first-run pagination (they avoid stranded cues and bottom headings but split long action anywhere).
- Un-omitting a scene restores its text exactly but not revision marks that were inside it (the restored elements are new to the sidecar). Marks made afterwards behave normally.
- PDF export of revision asterisks and dual columns is proven by reading the PDF back in tests, not by a human eye yet; worth a glance during your first real pass.
- Corkboard and pacing cards show locked scene NUMBERS but their page lengths remain unlocked estimates.
