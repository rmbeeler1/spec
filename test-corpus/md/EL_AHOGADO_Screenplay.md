## ACT ONE

### SCENE 1

**INT. SAINT MERCY HOSPITAL - ONCOLOGY WING - DAY**

Fluorescent lights hum. The walls are that particular shade of institutional green that exists nowhere else on Earth.

DERRICK COLE (late 30s) sits in a plastic chair designed for discomfort. He's got the kind of face that's seen violence—a scar through his left eyebrow, hands that look like they've broken things. But right now, those hands are clasped together, white-knuckled, like a man in prayer.

Across from him sits DR. ELENA VASQUEZ (50s), silver-streaked hair pulled back severely. Her desk is immaculate. Her expression is carefully neutral.

**DR. VASQUEZ**
The scans confirm what we suspected. The tumor has metastasized to Mike's lymphatic system.

Derrick doesn't move. Doesn't blink.

**DR. VASQUEZ (CONT'D)**
Without intervention, we're looking at six months. Maybe less.

**DERRICK**
*(voice flat)*
And with intervention?

**DR. VASQUEZ**
Standard treatment... radiation, chemotherapy... might buy us a year. Eighteen months if we're lucky.

**DERRICK**
That's not good enough.

**DR. VASQUEZ**
Mr. Cole—

**DERRICK**
There has to be something else. Something better.

Dr. Vasquez hesitates. She pulls a tablet from her desk, scrolls through it.

**DR. VASQUEZ**
There is an experimental treatment. Gene therapy. Helix Biotech has had remarkable success with pediatric lymphoma cases. Ninety-two percent remission rate in trials.

Hope flickers across Derrick's face. Then dies.

**DERRICK**
Helix.

**DR. VASQUEZ**
They're the only provider. The treatment isn't covered by any government program. It's... expensive.

**DERRICK**
How expensive?

She turns the tablet toward him. The number on the screen might as well be written in another language.

**$847,000**

Derrick stares at it. His jaw tightens.

**DR. VASQUEZ**
I'm sorry. I know that's not what you wanted to hear. If there was another way—

**DERRICK**
There isn't.

He stands. Pauses at the door.

**DERRICK (CONT'D)**
How long do I have? Before it's too late for the treatment to work?

**DR. VASQUEZ**
Three weeks. Maybe four. After that, the disease will have progressed too far.

Derrick nods once. Walks out.

---

### SCENE 2

**INT. DERRICK'S APARTMENT - LIVING ROOM - NIGHT**

A small apartment in a building that's seen better decades. Everything is worn but clean. A few photographs on the walls—military unit shots, a young Derrick with a man who looks like him (MARCUS), a school picture of a smiling boy.

Derrick sits at a folding table covered in paper. Bills. Collection notices. A laptop showing a bank account with three figures that should have four.

He's on the phone. The conversation is clearly not going well.

**DERRICK**
*(into phone)*
I understand the policy, but I was told my application was approved... No, I don't have additional collateral... Yes, I know what collateral means.

He listens. His free hand forms a fist.

**DERRICK (CONT'D)**
I'm not asking for a gift. I'm asking for a loan. I have work history. I have—

*(beat)*

He pulls the phone away from his ear. Looks at it. Disconnected.

**DERRICK (CONT'D)**
Son of a bitch.

He sets the phone down carefully. Takes a breath. Then sweeps the papers off the table in one violent motion.

They scatter across the floor. He stands there, breathing hard.

A door opens behind him.

**MIKE (O.S.)**
Uncle D?

Derrick spins. MIKE (10) stands in the doorway to the bedroom. Thin. Pale. A blue knit cap covering a head that used to have hair. Despite everything, there's something bright in his eyes. Trust.

Derrick immediately drops to one knee, face transforming into something gentler.

**DERRICK**
Hey, buddy. What are you doing up?

**MIKE**
I heard a noise.

**DERRICK**
Yeah, I... I dropped some stuff. Clumsy.

Mike looks at the papers scattered across the floor. He's ten, not stupid.

**MIKE**
Are you okay?

**DERRICK**
I'm fine. Come on, let's get you back to bed.

He guides Mike back toward the bedroom. Mike resists slightly.

**MIKE**
Uncle D?

**DERRICK**
Yeah?

**MIKE**
Are we gonna be okay?

Derrick crouches down to Mike's level. Looks him in the eyes.

**DERRICK**
Yeah. We're gonna be okay. I promise.

**MIKE**
Dad used to say that. Before.

Derrick's face tightens. The ghost of Marcus, always present.

**DERRICK**
Your dad was a good man. And he meant it when he said it. So do I.

**MIKE**
I know.

Mike hugs him. Derrick holds on like he's drowning.

**MIKE (CONT'D)**
*(muffled against Derrick's shoulder)*
I love you, Uncle D.

**DERRICK**
I love you too, buddy.

He picks Mike up—the kid is light, too light—and carries him back to bed.

---

### SCENE 3

**INT. DERRICK'S APARTMENT - MIKE'S BEDROOM - CONTINUOUS**

A small room with superhero posters and a shelf of well-worn books. A nightlight casts soft shadows.

Derrick tucks Mike in. Adjusts the covers.

**MIKE**
Will you tell me a story?

**DERRICK**
It's late, buddy.

**MIKE**
Please? One of Dad's stories. About the swamps.

Derrick hesitates. Those stories. The ones Marcus used to tell, sanitized versions of their runs through the wetlands. Adventures with boats and hidden treasure and mysterious islands.

**DERRICK**
Alright. One story.

He sits on the edge of the bed.

**DERRICK (CONT'D)**
Did I ever tell you about the time your dad and I found the ghost ship?

Mike shakes his head, already smiling.

**DERRICK (CONT'D)**
We were out past the barrier islands, way further than we should've been. Middle of the night, fog so thick you could cut it with a knife...

As he tells the story, his voice softens. Mike's eyes grow heavy.

**DERRICK (CONT'D)**
...and there it was. Rising out of the mist like something from another world. Your dad, he wanted to go aboard. He was always the brave one.

Mike is asleep. Derrick stops talking. Watches his nephew breathe.

**DERRICK (CONT'D)**
*(whispered)*
I'm gonna fix this, Mike. Whatever it takes.

He stands. Turns off the light. Pauses at the door.

In the darkness, his face changes. The gentle uncle disappears. Something harder takes its place. A decision being made.

---

### SCENE 4

**EXT. WATERFRONT DISTRICT - NIGHT**

A part of the city that never fully recovered from the flooding. Buildings that were once prime real estate now stand half-empty, water stains marking how high the tide once reached.

Derrick walks through the rain, collar up, hands in pockets. He passes people who don't want to be seen—deals happening in doorways, figures huddled under overhangs.

He stops at a nondescript building. No sign. No markings. Just a door with a camera above it.

He stands there. Letting the camera see him.

The door buzzes open.

---

### SCENE 5

**INT. FIXER'S OFFICE - CONTINUOUS**

A space that pretends to be legitimate. Leather furniture, a mahogany desk, even a small bookshelf. But the windows are blacked out and the air smells like money that was earned in darkness.

THE FIXER (50s) sits behind the desk. Gray hair, gray suit, gray eyes. Everything about him is deliberately forgettable.

**THE FIXER**
Mr. Cole. Please, sit.

Derrick remains standing.

**DERRICK**
You called me.

**THE FIXER**
I did. Helix Biotech is always looking for talented individuals.

**DERRICK**
I'm not interested.

**THE FIXER**
Really? Because I understand you have a nephew in need of medical treatment. Treatment that only Helix can provide.

Derrick's face hardens.

**DERRICK**
How do you know about Mike?

**THE FIXER**
We know about everything, Mr. Cole. It's our business to know. Please. Sit.

This time, Derrick sits.

**THE FIXER (CONT'D)**
Your record is impressive. Military service—commendations, deployments I'm not supposed to know about. Then private sector work. Security consulting. All above board.

**DERRICK**
I went straight.

**THE FIXER**
You did. Very admirable.

He opens a folder on his desk.

**THE FIXER (CONT'D)**
But before that... you operated in the wetlands. Smuggling operations. Quite successful, from what I understand. Until your brother's unfortunate death.

**DERRICK**
You don't get to talk about my brother.

**THE FIXER**
My apologies. I merely mention it to establish that you have... relevant experience. Experience that Helix requires.

**DERRICK**
For what?

**THE FIXER**
Asset recovery. There's a facility in the Miami wetlands. It was abandoned during the evacuation. It contains materials of significant value to Helix. We need a team to retrieve them.

**DERRICK**
The wetlands are off-limits. Government enforced.

**THE FIXER**
The government enforces the border. What happens inside... that's between the warlords and anyone brave enough to enter. Or desperate enough.

**DERRICK**
What's the catch?

**THE FIXER**
Two of our people must accompany the team. A scientist, to identify and secure the materials. And security, to protect our investment.

**DERRICK**
Corporate handlers.

**THE FIXER**
If you prefer that term. They'll have operational authority regarding the objective. Everything else is your discretion.

**DERRICK**
And compensation?

**THE FIXER**
Two hundred thousand dollars per team member upon successful completion.

Derrick doesn't react.

**THE FIXER (CONT'D)**
Plus, of course, full medical coverage for your nephew. The gene therapy. All associated costs. Helix will cover everything.

Now Derrick reacts. Just a flicker—hope and suspicion warring.

**DERRICK**
Why me? You could hire any crew.

**THE FIXER**
We did.

Beat.

**DERRICK**
What happened to them?

**THE FIXER**
Communication was lost. We assume structural collapse. The facility has been underwater for years.

**DERRICK**
You assume.

**THE FIXER**
The wetlands are dangerous, Mr. Cole. That's why we need someone with your specific expertise. Your knowledge of the territory. Your ability to assemble a competent team.

Derrick stands. Walks to the window, even though there's nothing to see through the blackout glass.

**DERRICK**
I left that life for a reason.

**THE FIXER**
I understand. But circumstances change. Principles are a luxury for people without dying nephews.

Derrick turns sharply.

**THE FIXER (CONT'D)**
I apologize if that was crass. But it's true, isn't it? You're here because you've exhausted every other option. The banks won't help. Government assistance won't cover it. And Mike's condition is deteriorating.

He slides a tablet across the desk. Medical charts. Mike's charts.

**THE FIXER (CONT'D)**
Three weeks. Maybe four. That's what Dr. Vasquez said, correct?

Derrick stares at the tablet. At his nephew's death sentence rendered in graphs and numbers.

**DERRICK**
You're bastards. All of you.

**THE FIXER**
Perhaps. But we're bastards who can save your nephew. Take the job, Mr. Cole. Bring us what we need. And Mike lives.

Long pause. Derrick's shoulders drop slightly. The fight going out of him.

**DERRICK**
When?

**THE FIXER**
The team departs in four days. Time enough to assemble your crew and prepare.

**DERRICK**
I'll need to see the facility specs. Maps. Intel on current warlord territories.

**THE FIXER**
Everything you need will be provided.

He produces a small envelope, slides it across.

**THE FIXER (CONT'D)**
Front money. For expenses.

Derrick takes it. Weighs it in his hand. Dirty money. Blood money.

Money that will save Mike's life.

**DERRICK**
I'll need five people besides your handlers. My choice. My crew.

**THE FIXER**
As I said—your discretion.

Derrick heads for the door.

**THE FIXER (CONT'D)**
Mr. Cole.

Derrick stops.

**THE FIXER (CONT'D)**
Don't think about running. About taking the money and disappearing. We will find you. And Mike will die in a government ward, alone, wondering why his uncle abandoned him.

Derrick turns. There's something dangerous in his eyes now.

**DERRICK**
Threaten my nephew again, and I'll kill you. Corporate backing or not.

The Fixer smiles. Like he expected nothing less.

**THE FIXER**
Four days, Mr. Cole. Don't be late.

### SCENE 6

**EXT. INDUSTRIAL MARINA - NIGHT**

Boats of all sizes, most of them held together by rust and prayer. This is where the smugglers dock, away from the legal marinas with their fees and inspections.

Derrick walks the pier. He stops at a mid-sized vessel—fast, low profile, obviously modified. THE BITTER END is painted on the stern in faded letters.

REEVES (late 30s/early 40s) is on deck, coiling rope. He's got the weathered look of a man who's lived his life outdoors, in dangerous places. A scar curves along his jaw.

**REEVES**
*(without looking up)*
Thought I smelled something straight. What brings you to the docks, Derrick?

**DERRICK**
I need to talk.

**REEVES**
So talk.

Derrick steps onto the boat. Reeves finally looks up. Takes in Derrick's face.

**REEVES (CONT'D)**
Oh. That kind of talk.

He sets down the rope. Gestures below.

**REEVES (CONT'D)**
Come on, then. Let's hear it.

---

### SCENE 7

**INT. THE BITTER END - CABIN - CONTINUOUS**

Small but functional. Maps on the walls, half of them showing wetland territories. A bottle of whiskey and two glasses appear.

Reeves pours. Slides one to Derrick.

**REEVES**
It's Mike, isn't it?

**DERRICK**
How'd you know?

**REEVES**
Only thing that'd bring you back. Marcus's boy.

Derrick takes a drink.

**DERRICK**
He's dying. Cancer. There's a treatment, but...

**REEVES**
Let me guess. Helix has it, and they want something in return.

**DERRICK**
Retrieval job. Wetlands. An old facility in the Miami grid.

**REEVES**
Which grid?

**DERRICK**
Sector 7.

Reeves whistles low.

**REEVES**
That's deep. Warlord country.

**DERRICK**
I know.

**REEVES**
What happened to the last team?

**DERRICK**
"Structural collapse." That's what they're saying.

**REEVES**
But you don't believe it.

**DERRICK**
Would you?

Reeves leans back.

**REEVES**
What're they paying?

**DERRICK**
Two hundred a head. Plus Mike's treatment.

**REEVES**
Corporate rates. They're scared.

**DERRICK**
Yeah.

**REEVES**
And you're going anyway.

**DERRICK**
I don't have a choice, Reeves. It's Mike.

Silence. Reeves stares at Derrick for a long moment.

**REEVES**
Marcus would've done the same. For any of us.

**DERRICK**
He would've.

**REEVES**
Goddamn it.

He downs his whiskey.

**REEVES (CONT'D)**
Alright. I'm in.

**DERRICK**
Just like that?

**REEVES**
Just like that. You think I could look in a mirror if I let you go alone? After Marcus?

The weight of a dead man hangs between them.

**DERRICK**
I need more than just you. Full crew. Navigator who knows explosives. Someone for salvage work.

**REEVES**
I've got a guy. Jax. He's good. Learned demo in the army before he wised up.

**DERRICK**
What about Malik?

**REEVES**
Malik... yeah, he's still around. Runs salvage on the edges. Owes you, doesn't he?

**DERRICK**
Pulled him out of a tight spot couple years back.

**REEVES**
Then he'll come. What about your private sector people? That guy you worked with—Torres?

**DERRICK**
I was thinking the same thing. He's got skills we need.

**REEVES**
When do we leave?

**DERRICK**
Four days.

**REEVES**
Gonna be tight. I'll handle Jax. You get the others.

Derrick nods. Stands to leave. Pauses.

**DERRICK**
Reeves. Thank you.

**REEVES**
Don't thank me yet. Thank me when we're back and Mike's getting better.

---

### SCENE 8

**INT. SALVAGE WAREHOUSE - DAY**

A massive space full of recovered equipment, stripped machinery, and organized chaos. Somewhere between a junkyard and an archaeological dig.

MALIK (30s) is elbow-deep in a piece of equipment, tools scattered around him. He's got an easy smile and an energy that suggests he'd be optimistic during a funeral.

Derrick enters. Malik looks up, squints.

**MALIK**
Holy shit. Derrick Cole. I thought you were dead or born again.

**DERRICK**
Neither. Just reformed.

**MALIK**
Same thing in this business.

He wipes his hands, comes over. They clasp hands, brief shoulder bump.

**MALIK (CONT'D)**
What brings the prodigal son back to my humble establishment?

**DERRICK**
Job offer.

**MALIK**
Legal or illegal?

**DERRICK**
Does it matter?

**MALIK**
Not really. Just like to know what I'm lying about.

Derrick smiles despite himself.

**MALIK (CONT'D)**
What kind of job?

**DERRICK**
Wetlands. Deep penetration. Asset recovery.

**MALIK**
Deep penetration. Asset recovery. You corporate types and your euphemisms. Just say "stealing shit from underwater."

**DERRICK**
Two hundred thousand.

Malik stops joking.

**MALIK**
That's... that's a real number.

**DERRICK**
It's a real job. And it's dangerous. I won't lie to you.

**MALIK**
You never did.

He walks to a workbench. Picks up a tool. Sets it down. Thinking.

**MALIK (CONT'D)**
How dangerous are we talking?

**DERRICK**
Previous team didn't come back.

**MALIK**
Jesus.

**DERRICK**
I know. That's why I need people I trust. People who know what they're doing.

**MALIK**
You trust me?

**DERRICK**
You're still alive. In this business, that's a resume.

Malik laughs.

**MALIK**
Fair point.

He drums his fingers on the workbench.

**MALIK (CONT'D)**
Two hundred thousand.

**DERRICK**
Clean. Helix Biotech.

**MALIK**
Helix. Those parasites.

**DERRICK**
Yeah. But their money spends.

**MALIK**
Who else is in?

**DERRICK**
Reeves. He's bringing someone for navigation and demo. I'm recruiting Torres.

**MALIK**
Reeves. That's good. He's hard to kill.

Beat.

**MALIK (CONT'D)**
Ah, what the hell. I'm in.

**DERRICK**
You sure?

**MALIK**
Man, look at this place. I've been scraping by on salvage scraps for three years. Two hundred grand? I could retire. Or at least upgrade to scraping by in comfort.

He grins.

**MALIK (CONT'D)**
When do we leave?

**DERRICK**
Four days. I'll send coordinates for the meetup.

**MALIK**
I'll bring the good toys.

---

### SCENE 9

**INT. CORPORATE OFFICE BUILDING - DAY**

Clean lines, glass walls, the smell of printer toner and ambition. A complete contrast to the places Derrick has been.

He walks through a cubicle farm, drawing stares. He doesn't fit here. Never did.

TORRES (late 30s) spots him from his desk. His face goes through several expressions—surprise, concern, wariness. He rises, intercepts Derrick.

**TORRES**
*(quietly)*
What the hell are you doing here?

**DERRICK**
We need to talk.

**TORRES**
We talk on the phone like normal people.

**DERRICK**
Not this conversation.

Torres looks around. Co-workers are watching.

**TORRES**
Break room. Two minutes.

---

### SCENE 10

**INT. CORPORATE OFFICE - BREAK ROOM - CONTINUOUS**

Vending machines, a sad coffee maker, motivational posters. Torres closes the door.

**TORRES**
You can't just show up at my job, Derrick.

**DERRICK**
I know. I'm sorry.

**TORRES**
Then why are you here?

**DERRICK**
Because I need you. For a job.

Torres takes a step back.

**TORRES**
No. No, we talked about this. We both got out. That was the whole point.

**DERRICK**
I know—

**TORRES**
I have a wife now. Kids. A mortgage that actually gets paid on time. I'm not throwing that away to go back to... to whatever you've gotten yourself into.

**DERRICK**
It's Mike.

Torres stops.

**TORRES**
Mike? Marcus's kid?

**DERRICK**
He's sick. Cancer. There's a treatment, but I can't afford it. Not legally.

**TORRES**
Jesus.

**DERRICK**
Helix has what he needs. They'll provide it if I do this job for them.

**TORRES**
What kind of job?

**DERRICK**
Wetlands retrieval.

Torres runs his hands over his face.

**TORRES**
The wetlands. Of course.

**DERRICK**
Two hundred thousand per person. Plus Mike's treatment. One job, Torres. That's all.

**TORRES**
How many times have we heard "one job"?

**DERRICK**
I know how it sounds. But it's true. I do this, Mike lives, and I never touch this life again.

**TORRES**
What if something goes wrong?

**DERRICK**
Then I die trying to save my nephew. And at least I tried.

Torres is quiet for a long moment. He looks at the door. At the life on the other side of it.

**TORRES**
Maria would kill me.

**DERRICK**
Then don't tell her. Four days. I'll have you back in a week.

**TORRES**
A week.

**DERRICK**
Week and a half at most.

Torres laughs bitterly.

**TORRES**
Why do I feel like I'm about to make the stupidest decision of my life?

**DERRICK**
Because you are. But I wouldn't ask if I had any other choice.

**TORRES**
I know. That's why I'm going to say yes.

He shakes his head.

**TORRES (CONT'D)**
God help me. I'm in.

**DERRICK**
Thank you, Torres.

**TORRES**
Don't thank me. Just get me home in one piece.

---

### SCENE 11

**INT. WAREHOUSE - STAGING AREA - DAY**

An abandoned warehouse on the edge of the legal zone. The team has gathered: DERRICK, REEVES, MALIK, TORRES, and JAX.

JAX (mid-late 20s) is younger than the others, but carries himself with quiet competence. He's laying out equipment on a tarp—explosive charges, detonators, navigation gear.

The team checks weapons, gear, supplies. There's the nervous energy of men about to do something dangerous.

**MALIK**
*(examining equipment)*
Reeves, where'd you find this kid? He's got better toys than me.

**JAX**
Military surplus. And I'm not a kid.

**MALIK**
You're what, twenty-five? That's a kid.

**JAX**
Twenty-seven. And I've got more time in combat zones than you've got in whatever you call that salvage operation.

**MALIK**
Ooh. He's got teeth.

**REEVES**
Jax is good, Malik. Saved my ass twice in the outer territories. Trust him.

**TORRES**
*(to Derrick, quietly)*
What do we actually know about this facility?

**DERRICK**
Meridian Genetics research campus. Seven buildings, partially submerged. Abandoned during the evacuation twelve years ago.

**TORRES**
Meridian. They do gene therapy, right?

**DERRICK**
Among other things.

**TORRES**
Why would Helix want to recover from a competitor's facility?

**DERRICK**
They said it was abandoned corporate assets. Previous Helix property transferred during a merger or something.

Torres looks skeptical.

**TORRES**
And you believed that?

**DERRICK**
I didn't ask too many questions.

The warehouse door opens. Everyone tenses. Hands move toward weapons.

Two figures enter. SIMON (mid-30s) in expensive tactical gear that's clearly never seen use, and SHAW (40s), moving with the precision of a career soldier.

Simon looks around the warehouse with barely concealed distaste. Shaw scans the room, evaluating threats.

**SIMON**
Mr. Cole, I presume?

**DERRICK**
You must be our handlers.

**SIMON**
I prefer "corporate liaisons." Simon Chen. Research and development. This is Shaw. Security.

Shaw nods once, says nothing.

**REEVES**
Research and development needs security?

**SIMON**
Helix believes in protecting its investments. Mr. Shaw is here to ensure my safety. And to provide additional tactical support.

**REEVES**
What's your combat experience?

**SIMON**
I have extensive laboratory experience—

**REEVES**
So none.

**SIMON**
Mr. Shaw handles the tactical considerations. I handle the objective.

**MALIK**
*(to Torres)*
Fifty bucks says the suit doesn't make it past day one.

**TORRES**
I don't take sucker bets.

**DERRICK**
Let's get one thing clear. Once we're in the wetlands, my word is law. We move when I say, stop when I say, and if I tell you to run, you run.

**SIMON**
Within reason—

**DERRICK**
There's no "within reason" in the wetlands. You listen or you die. Those are the options.

Simon and Derrick lock eyes. A battle of wills.

**SHAW**
*(first words)*
Understood.

Simon glances at Shaw, surprised. Shaw gives him a look—*this is not the hill to die on*.

**SIMON**
Fine. You have operational authority. But the objective remains my priority. We don't leave until I have what we came for.

**DERRICK**
What exactly are we coming for?

**SIMON**
Research materials. Data storage devices. Some biological samples.

**TORRES**
Samples of what?

**SIMON**
That's need-to-know.

**MALIK**
We're the ones going in. We need to know.

**SIMON**
You need to know how to get us there and back. I'll handle the retrieval.

Tension fills the room.

**REEVES**
Derrick. A word.

Derrick follows Reeves to a corner.

**REEVES (CONT'D)**
*(low)*
I don't like this. They're hiding something.

**DERRICK**
I know.

**REEVES**
Then why are we doing this?

**DERRICK**
Because Mike is dying. And this is the only way.

**REEVES**
Even if it kills us?

**DERRICK**
Even then.

Reeves studies his friend's face. Sees something that can't be argued with.

**REEVES**
Alright. But when this goes sideways—

**DERRICK**
When?

**REEVES**
Brother, it always goes sideways. When it does, I've got your back. But I won't die for that corporate suit.

**DERRICK**
Fair enough.

They return to the group.

**DERRICK (CONT'D)**
We leave at dawn. Three boats—distributed assets in case of problems. Reeves pilots the lead, I've got second, Torres on third.

**JAX**
Comm protocols?

**DERRICK**
Encrypted radio, line of sight when possible. Full blackout once we're past the warlord checkpoints.

**SIMON**
We'll need to pass through Kross's territory. I've arranged payment.

**MALIK**
Kross? That's the reasonable one.

**REEVES**
"Reasonable" is relative in the wetlands.

**DERRICK**
Everyone get some sleep. We'll do final equipment check at 0400.

The team disperses. Simon approaches Derrick.

**SIMON**
I understand you have... concerns about this mission.

**DERRICK**
Do you?

**SIMON**
Helix appreciates your skepticism. It's kept you alive. But I want you to know—the mission is straightforward. We go in, we retrieve the materials, we leave. Nothing exotic.

**DERRICK**
Then why did the last team die?

Simon's face flickers. Just for a moment.

**SIMON**
Structural instability. These facilities have been underwater for over a decade. We've provided updated schematics. Better equipment.

**DERRICK**
And if it wasn't structural instability?

**SIMON**

What now?

**DERRICK**
I don't know. Try to go straight again, I guess. For real this time.

**REEVES**
Think you can?

**DERRICK**
I have to. Can't keep going back to that world. Not anymore.

**REEVES**
What about when the money runs out? When Mike needs something else Helix can provide?

**DERRICK**
I'll figure it out.

**REEVES**
That's not an answer.

**DERRICK**
It's the only one I have.

They drink.

**REEVES**
Torres went home. Said Maria didn't ask too many questions.

**DERRICK**
She knows.

**REEVES**
Probably. But she loves him enough not to push.

**DERRICK**
What about you?

**REEVES**
Back to the boat. Back to the runs.

**DERRICK**
After everything?

**REEVES**
It's all I know, Derrick. All I'm good at.

**DERRICK**
That's not true.

**REEVES**
Isn't it?

He finishes his beer.

**REEVES (CONT'D)**
At least now I know where not to go. Sector 7. El Ahogado's territory.

**DERRICK**
It'll spread. If Helix keeps poking at it.

**REEVES**
Maybe. Maybe not. But that's not our problem anymore.

**DERRICK**
No. I guess it's not.

The sun sinks lower. The sky turns purple.

**REEVES**
Derrick.

**DERRICK**
Yeah?

**REEVES**
We did the right thing. Whatever it cost... we did the right thing.

**DERRICK**
Did we?

**REEVES**
Mike's alive. We're alive. Malik's going to make it.

**DERRICK**
Shaw's dead. Jax is dead. The first team is dead.

**REEVES**
And none of that is on you. It's on Helix. On Meridian. On every corporation that plays God and lets the little people clean up the mess.

Derrick says nothing.

**REEVES (CONT'D)**
You did what you had to do. For family. That's all any of us can do.

**DERRICK**
Marcus would've said the same thing.

**REEVES**
He would've.

They sit in silence as the sun disappears.

**DERRICK**
You know... if you ever need anything. If you ever want to get out...

**REEVES**
I know where to find you.

They clasp hands. Brothers in all but blood.

**REEVES (CONT'D)**
Take care of yourself, Derrick.

**DERRICK**
You too, Reeves.

Reeves stands. Walks away down the beach.

Derrick watches him go. Then turns back to the water.

Calm. Beautiful. Nothing like the wetlands.

But in his mind, he's still there. Still running. Still seeing Jax disappear into those jaws. Still feeling the water rise.

Some things you never escape.

### SCENE 56

**EXT. MIAMI WETLANDS - TWILIGHT**

The ruined facility in the distance. Broken buildings rising from still water. The last light of day painting everything gold.

A DRONE moves through the air, camera recording. A Helix logo visible on its side.

It circles the facility. Recording the damage. The collapsed walkway. The debris.

The drone descends, camera aimed at the water.

Still. Dark. Impenetrable.

The drone hovers. Watching.

A ripple. Nothing more than a disturbance in the surface. Could be anything.

The drone's light pierces the water.

There—deep below—something glows. Faint. Bioluminescent.

Then nothing.

Just still water. Shadows. The debris of human ambition.

The drone continues its survey. Finds nothing definitive.

But something is there. Watching the machine move across its territory.

Patient. Eternal. Waiting.

### SCENE 57

**INT. HELIX BIOTECH - RESEARCH LAB - NIGHT**

Simon stands before a bank of monitors. Data scrolling. Charts and graphs.

He's different now. Thinner. Darker circles under his eyes. He doesn't sleep well anymore.

The HELIX EXECUTIVE enters.

**HELIX EXECUTIVE**
The preliminary analysis is impressive.

**SIMON**
It's more than impressive. It's revolutionary.

He pulls up an image—the creature, reconstructed from footage.

**SIMON (CONT'D)**
Enhanced regeneration. Adaptive intelligence. Sensory capabilities beyond anything in nature.

**HELIX EXECUTIVE**
Can we replicate it?

**SIMON**
Given time. The genetic sequences are complex, but...

He trails off. Stares at the image.

**HELIX EXECUTIVE**
Dr. Chen?

**SIMON**
You've seen what it can do. What it did.

**HELIX EXECUTIVE**
I've seen the reports.

**SIMON**
Reports don't capture it. The intelligence in its eyes. The patience. It didn't just kill—it hunted. Calculated.

**HELIX EXECUTIVE**
That's what makes it valuable.

**SIMON**
That's what makes it terrifying.

The executive steps closer.

**HELIX EXECUTIVE**
Doctor, your concerns are noted. But this research represents a significant competitive advantage for Helix.

**SIMON**
Meridian thought the same thing. Look where it got them.

**HELIX EXECUTIVE**
Meridian made mistakes. We won't.

**SIMON**
That's what they said.

He turns to face the executive.

**SIMON (CONT'D)**
I've been thinking. About what Cole said. About whether some things should stay buried.

**HELIX EXECUTIVE**
Cole is a hired contractor, not a scientist.

**SIMON**
He's also alive. Which is more than I can say for most people who've encountered that creature.

**HELIX EXECUTIVE**
Doctor, the decision has been made. Helix will proceed with the Gen-3 project. Your role is to make it happen.

**SIMON**
And if I refuse?

**HELIX EXECUTIVE**
(cold)
Then we'll find someone else. And you'll have to explain to any future employers why you left a multi-billion dollar corporation.

Simon stares at the screen. At the creature frozen in digital capture.

**SIMON**
(quiet)
We're making a mistake.

**HELIX EXECUTIVE**
That's above your pay grade, Doctor.

He walks out.

Simon stands alone in the lab. Watching the creature's image.

**SIMON**
(to himself)
We're making a terrible mistake.

### SCENE 58

**EXT. MIAMI WETLANDS - NIGHT**

The ruined facility. Moonlight on still water.

Deep below the surface, something moves. A shape, impossibly large, gliding through the depths.

It surfaces slowly. Silently. Just enough for those amber eyes to break the water.

Watching. Always watching.

The bioluminescent patches pulse. Slower now. Healing.

Around it, in the darkness: more glowing patches. Smaller. Newer.

Not one.

Several.

The creature's territory. Its domain.

And it's not alone.

The mother sinks back beneath the surface. Her young follow.

Patient. Growing. Learning.

El Ahogado endures.

---

### SCENE 59

**INT. DERRICK'S APARTMENT - NIGHT**

Derrick sits in the dark, watching Mike sleep.

The boy is healthier now. Color in his cheeks. Hair starting to grow back.

The treatment is working.

Derrick's phone buzzes. A text from a blocked number:

*"We could use someone with your experience. When you're ready."*

He deletes it.

Then stares out the window, at the city lights, at the ocean in the distance.

Somewhere out there, the wetlands wait. The creature waits.

And Helix is planning to go back.

**DERRICK**
(whispered)
Some things should stay buried.

He knows they won't listen. They never do.

And someday—maybe soon, maybe years from now—it will all come undone.

But not tonight. Tonight, his nephew is alive. His friends are safe. The nightmare is over.

For now.

### SCENE 60

**EXT. MIAMI WETLANDS - DAWN**

Final image.

The sun rises over the drowned city. Golden light on dark water.

The camera pushes slowly toward the Meridian facility. Past the debris. Past the warnings.

To the water's edge.

Still. Perfect.

Then—a ripple.

Something passes just beneath the surface.

A faint glow. There and gone.

Was it real? Or just a trick of the light?

The wetlands keep their secrets.

**FADE TO BLACK.**
